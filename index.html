    About

    ~/about

    I work on the investment data that portfolio managers rely on every day. As part of Davy’s Portfolio Insights team, I help maintain the quality of holdings, performance, exposure and risk data across our UCITS fund range and segregated portfolios. That data feeds client reporting, board and ManCo packs, and SAA reviews. Day to day I work in Jacobi, Snowflake, FactSet and Excel alongside our portfolio managers and quants.

    I also build. I use Claude to design AI-assisted workflows that take manual validation and reporting work off the team. Pair that with SQL and Python, and checks that took hours become repeatable, auditable processes.

    Before this, I spent two years on Davy’s Risk & Compliance Graduate Programme. In Financial Risk I produced daily VaR reporting and ran backtesting and stress tests for the Financial Risk Committee. In Capital Markets Compliance I covered MAR trade surveillance and CBI and ESMA regulatory submissions. One notable contribution, a systems integration for regulatory attestations, saved the firm €55k.

    More about me

    ~/more-about-me

    Interests

    I am passionate about investments, financial markets, and the application of AI and process automation in finance. I am actively building my knowledge across equity analysis, fixed income, derivatives and portfolio management.

    I am genuinely excited by the role AI and automation play in modern finance. Whether it is building Power Query workflows, automating compliance reporting with Power BI and Power Automate, or exploring how machine learning is reshaping investment decision making, I enjoy finding smarter ways to work with data.

    Tennis

    I have been playing tennis for over three years, and it has quickly become my passion. Through dedicated practice and competition, I have developed a strong appreciation for the sport’s strategy, agility and mental challenges.

    Tennis has allowed me to connect with others, build discipline and foster teamwork. The thrill of a well-played match and the satisfaction of improving my skills are what drive my enthusiasm for the game.

    Much like analysing data to uncover insights, I enjoy breaking down the complexities of tennis: anticipating shots, refining technique and adapting to each opponent. The court has become my arena for growth, learning and personal achievement.

    Tennis is an integral part of my life, a dynamic outlet for my competitive spirit and a way to express my determination and perseverance.

    Classical guitar

    I have been playing classical guitar for more than 8 years, specialising in classical Spanish songs. Some of my favourite pieces include Etude, Sagreras Zamba and the iconic Asturias by Isaac Albéniz.

    I have performed in numerous concerts, most notably at Emanuel within the Helix. Sharing my passion for the guitar with an audience has been truly rewarding, connecting with others through the universal language of music.

    Much like turning data into valuable insights, I find joy in unravelling musical notation and translating it into emotive performances. Understanding the nuances of each piece, its harmony, rhythm and melody, is like solving an intricate puzzle.

    Music is an integral part of my life: a creative outlet, and a way to express my thoughts and feelings.
