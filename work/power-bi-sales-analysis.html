Project P-05 · Dublin City University coursework

Power BI sales analysis

A business analytics dashboard for Blackbird, a company seeking insight from its sales data, so it can analyse its sales performance and make data-driven decisions for growth.

Code
Repository on GitHub
Credit
MT412 group project
Stack
Power BI

01 What it is

The dashboard breaks down individual orders: customer information, location, order amounts, sales, product quantities and profit margins. Blackbird can track sales trends, identify top-selling products and monitor profitability across regions and timeframes.

02 Method

  • Geographic reach. A coloured sales-volume map of the United States shows the strongest and weakest states; a filled map shows the most popular product sub-category in each state.
  • Operations. Q&A for quick data exploration, cards for top-selling products by category, charts of the most common shipping methods, and an analysis of how average discount rates affect sales.
  • Customers. Customer data (ID, name, location) supports segmentation, so marketing and sales strategies can be tailored.

03 Results

An interactive dashboard that gives Blackbird a comprehensive view of its sales data, supporting data-driven decisions, strategic planning and improvements to logistics, pricing and promotions.

04 Tools

  • Power BI
  • Q&A visual
  • Filled and bubble maps
  • Cards
